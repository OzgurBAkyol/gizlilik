Loopboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Your photos and videos never leave your device. Collages are built and exported entirely on your iPhone. The developer has no server and receives none of your media.

No account needed. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.

No analytics or tracking. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs.

1. What the app processes

DataWhyWhere it stays
Photos, videos and Live Photos you pickTo place them in your collageCopied into the app's own storage on your device
Your collage projects (layout, crop, loop ranges)So you can keep editingOn your device
Monthly free-collage counterTo apply the free-plan limitOn your device

Face and subject detection (used to keep faces in frame when cropping) runs on your device with Apple's Vision framework. No image is uploaded anywhere.

2. Photo library access

Loopboard uses Apple's photo picker, so it only sees the items you choose. Saving a finished collage to Photos asks for "add only" permission; the app cannot read your library through it.

3. Data that leaves your device

None. Sharing a collage (Instagram, X, Messages…) happens only when you tap Share, through the iOS share sheet, to the app you choose.

4. Purchases

Loopboard Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Your payment details go to Apple; the developer never sees or stores them. The app only learns whether a subscription is active. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.

5. Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children; no data is collected at all.

6. Your rights

Your rights under GDPR and KVKK are reserved. Since everything is stored on your device, deleting the app deletes all of its data.

7. Changes

If the app's data behavior changes (for example if advertising is added), this page will be updated and the date above changed before that version is released.
